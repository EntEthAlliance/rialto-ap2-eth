This repository is an Enterprise Ethereum Alliance R&D project. All contributions are governed by the [EEA Intellectual Property Rights Policy](https://entethalliance.org/governing-documents/).

## Code contributions

Software contributions require an executed **EEA Contributor License Agreement (CLA)** on file **before merge** (EEA IPR Policy § 4.1(a)). If you do not have a CLA on file, contact the EEA via [entethalliance.org](https://entethalliance.org) or your EEA member representative before opening a pull request with code.

Third-party code may only be incorporated without EEA Board approval if it is licensed under one of the pre-approved licenses (IPR Policy § 4.1(b)): Apache License 2.0, BSD 3-Clause, BSD 2-Clause, or MIT.

## Specification contributions

Written contributions to the specification text (issues, pull requests against `README.md`, working-group input) are licensed to the EEA automatically under IPR Policy § 3.2 at the moment they are made. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions, subject to that license. If you are not the sole copyright owner of a contribution, identify the other owners in your submission.

Communications conspicuously marked “Not a Contribution” are excluded, per the EEA Contributor License Agreement definitions.
