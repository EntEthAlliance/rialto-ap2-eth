# Contributing

This repository publishes individual research by Redwan Meslem, Executive Director of the Enterprise Ethereum Alliance. The research is not an EEA standard, position, member work product, or statement of member consensus. Because the repository is administered by the EEA, contributions are governed by the [EEA Intellectual Property Rights Policy](https://entethalliance.org/governing-documents/).

`SPEC.md` is the canonical technical document. A pull request that changes scope, requirements, wire behavior, or security assumptions must update `SPEC.md`. The README, published page, and demo must remain consistent with it.

## Code contributions

Software contributions require an executed **EEA Contributor License Agreement (CLA)** on file **before merge** (EEA IPR Policy § 4.1(a)). If you do not have a CLA on file, contact the EEA via [entethalliance.org](https://entethalliance.org) or your EEA member representative before opening a pull request with code.

Third-party code may only be incorporated without EEA Board approval if it is licensed under one of the pre-approved licenses (IPR Policy § 4.1(b)): Apache License 2.0, BSD 3-Clause, BSD 2-Clause, or MIT.

## Specification contributions

Written contributions to the specification text (issues, pull requests against `SPEC.md` or explanatory documentation, and working-group input) are licensed to the EEA automatically under IPR Policy § 3.2 at the moment they are made. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions, subject to that license. If you are not the sole copyright owner of a contribution, identify the other owners in your submission.

Technical proposals should include:

- the dependency version or source commit they rely on;
- the exact section and decision they change;
- positive and negative vectors where byte or verification behavior changes; and
- a clear distinction between an upstream standard, a proposal, and local policy.
